ALL ABOUT CHRONOLOGY

Our Legacy in Chronology

In the age of science and technology, the world has been revolutionized. However, there has been some significant transformation in the watch industry, promoting advancement with a break from tradition and finally, an enhancement in the consumer’s journey. Yet, it has some negative impact on the environment as its add-ons the e-waste. There also has been loss of artistry in time and crafts as consumers seek advanced features compromising the artistry of the watch. Hence, the brand “SPAKAchrono” has been introduced to do justice to both the technologically innovated and undying classic watches.

À La Mode of Chronology

One of À la mode of smartwatches is Apple watch, Samsung watch, Garmin, Google Pixel, One Plus and so on as it provides traditional timekeeping look on display flexing the advancement in technology. There is a reason for having smartwatches to be the talk of the town, as it offers the users GPS, mobile notifications, fitness tracking, heart rate tracking, and sync with other e-devices which is desirable for technoids. In future, it is expected to have smartwatches with the touch of AI and machine learning to optimize the health features and other functions. Besides that, use of kinetic energy and solar energy could be the main source of the power for smartwatches which could sustain the smartness of the watch.

Tech-Enabled Business

No wonder, the internet and technology have made business connect with customers as a breeze. There are online platforms and ads that are singled out to reach the users who are looking for their desired chrono. On top of that AI can also track the ongoing trend and enhance the marketing strategies that can make SPAKAchrono competent. Blockchain technology has diminished the issue of losing warranty card and other purchasing details of the product. This can also help the user to find the authentic product without having to be skeptical of buying duplicate products. Wearable-as-a-Service models can also be introduced for commercial advertisements. This can fascinate Modish to get into premium products.

Obstacle of Advancement in Technology

SPAKAchrono is quite mindful of the users’ private and health data respecting the privacy and security of individuals with frequent software updates and yet, there is always a chance of data piracy. Smartwatches are prone to have shorter lifecycle in comparison as it might get outdated with time and ongoing trend. Besides that, every e-device has the disadvantage of degrading batteries. In addition, unused electronic devices are contributing to e-wastes which might be a huge environmental issue in future. Also, mining rare metals for the manufacture of the watches has both ethical and ecological complications. Besides that, the upgrading technology has been alienating traditional watch that inherits the heritage of ancient culture.
